Boat Towing Prototype

A physics sandbox: steer a boat towing an object on a rope, through a river current, obstacles, and a whirlpool.

Controls

Click/tap and hold anywhere on the canvas to steer the boat toward that point. The left panel sliders tune the physics live. Reset restarts the scene.

World

  • Boat — the vessel you steer.
  • Towed Object — dragged behind the boat by the rope.
  • Static — fixed obstacles.
  • Dynamic Obstacle — drifts along with the river current.
  • Whirlpool — pulls and swirls anything nearby.

Rope Gauge

The vertical bar tracks rope Tension (current pull), Threshold (the level that starts a snap countdown), Peak (highest tension reached), and Limit (the gauge's reference scale). Sustain tension above the threshold and the rope snaps.

Screen Wrap

Both the boat and the towed object wrap around the screen edges, Asteroids-style — a ghost preview appears near an edge before the crossing happens.
