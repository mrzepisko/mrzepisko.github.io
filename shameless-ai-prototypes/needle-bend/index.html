Needle Bend Prototype

A simulation of a flexible needle bending as it presses against a flat surface, rendered as a cubic Bézier curve driven entirely by cursor position.

How It Works

The tool's held end (p0, with the gray handle pinned to it) follows your cursor. The tip (p3) can never cross the baseline shown near the bottom edge. Once the tip touches that baseline, pressing further down feeds a value t from 0 (just touching, straight) to 1 (fully bent) over the Max Depth of overshoot — there's no clock involved, the shape is purely a function of how far you're currently pressing. The whole tool's orientation eases from 0 at rest to lying flat against the surface at full bend. The handle also gets its own small cosmetic tilt on top of that, blending the direction at p0 and at p1 per Angle Blend — purely visual, it never affects the needle's shape or position.

Controls

The left panel tunes the simulation live: the needle's base position, how far each bend control point is pulled and where it sits along the shaft, how deep a press it takes to reach full bend, and the handle's cosmetic angle blend. Enable Show Helpers to reveal the curve's four defining points (p0–p3) and the construction lines between them.
