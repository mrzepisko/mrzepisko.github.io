Fishing: Common Catch Prototype

Drag back, then release to cast - the bobber flies the opposite way, distance based on drag length.

Land near a school for a bite. Closer to center means a faster bite; miss the school entirely and you get junk instead.

Reel it in: hit the marker inside the blue zone to advance a round. The gold zone gives a bonus quality star if tagged in time - miss it and that round's star is gone for good. A miss elsewhere costs a life and slows the marker. Run out of time or lives and the fish escapes.
